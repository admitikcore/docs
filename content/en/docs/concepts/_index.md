A policy is a trigger, a context to evaluate and something to do with it. These pages cover the first
two, so the policy pages can stay about their own kind.

- [Trigger](/docs/concepts/trigger/): which resources make a policy run, and on which events.
- [Engines and context](/docs/concepts/engines-and-context/): what renders a template, and what it produces.
- [Sources and conditions](/docs/concepts/sources-and-conditions/): how to bring other objects in and decide with them.
- [Starlark](/docs/concepts/starlark/): the language and the modules available inside it.
